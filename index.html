JPG / JPEGPhotos and scansSmall files, slight quality loss. The default format of most phone cameras.
PNGScreenshots, text and graphicsSharp edges and text, larger file size. Supports transparency.
WEBPWeb imagesSmall files with good quality. Common when saving pictures from websites.

PDF Page Size and Orientation Explained

A4: A common paper size used in many countries. It is suitable for documents, forms, schoolwork and printing.

US Letter: A standard paper size commonly used in the United States and Canada.

Fit to image: Makes each PDF page match the dimensions of its image. This is useful for screenshots and photos that you do not want to crop or place inside a fixed-size page.

Portrait: A vertical page orientation, commonly used for documents.

Landscape: A horizontal page orientation, useful for wide images and screenshots.

Tips for Creating a Better PDF

  • Use clear, high-quality images for better results.
  • Photograph documents in good lighting and keep the camera straight above the page.
  • Arrange your images before conversion to get the correct PDF page order.
  • Use A4 for most standard documents.
  • Choose Fit to image if you want each PDF page to match the original image dimensions.
  • Check the upload size limit of the website where you plan to submit your PDF.

Troubleshooting

The PDF does not download

Check that your browser allows downloads from this site and that you have enough free storage. On iPhone, the file appears in the Files app or the Safari downloads list.

My image was not added

Only JPG, PNG and WEBP images are accepted, and up to 30 images at a time. Other formats such as HEIC should be exported as JPG first.

The conversion is slow or fails

Very large photos use a lot of memory. Try converting fewer images at once, or reduce the photo size before adding it.
